Description

Under general supervision, oversees the game day operations of community sports & facilities.

Examples of Duties


### Example Of Duties

Oversees the games day operations of community sports programs and facilities; uses authority with a common sense approach to effectively control games and spectators; communicates and maintains the respect of staff, coaches, parents, and participants; knows and interprets rules of play; makes decisions in a brisk, business-like manner; reacts intuitively to situations; knows and supports the aims and principals of youth and adult sports and the City’s youth activities philosophy; understands the comparable skills and limitations of the youth participating in the sports programs; preps field and maintains sites for sports programs; supervises officials and scorekeeping staff; officiates and score keeps if necessary; and performs additional duties as required.
