## Description

Airport Systems & Cloud Infrastructure Administrator

## Examples of Duties

Under general direction, is responsible for major sections of systems development, analysis and definition, systems and program specifications, and documentation of programs and systems.  Designs, develops, implements and maintains application software which supports complex business applications.  Participates in the design of new computer systems, modification of existing systems, feasability studies, analysis of business issues.  Works with Technical Services (DBAs, Networking, Hardware Support), customers, and user groups.  Defines system support needs and develops system requirements.  Participates in the design and architecture of complex systems with database application and web components.  Prepares training manuals related to information systems.  Supervises staff analyst.  Performs other job-related duties as required.

## Minimum Qualifications

Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physical Sciences, Engineering or closely related field from an accredited four (4) year college or university required.  Five (5) years of full-time paid experience in information processing, at least three (3) of which must include systems analysis in addition to programming, required.  Must have a knowledge of information processing including systems analysis and programming techniques.  Certification in Novell and NT is preferred.  In addition, one (1) year of supervisory experience required.  (Substitution:  Two (2) years of any equivalent combination of education, training and experience may be substituted for each year of college education lacking.)  Must have excellent written and verbal communication skills.  Valid State of Ohio Driver's License required.

Duties & Responsibilities

The Senior Systems Administrator is responsible is for responsible for advanced administration and engineering of the airport's Microsoft Windows environment, virtualization platform, and integrated IT systems.  This role ensures high availability, security, and compliance for critical airport operations technology, including AODB (Airport Operational Database), Baggage/loT systems.  Reporting to the Systems Operations Manager, the Senior Systems Administrator executes operational plans, drives automation, and provides Tier 2 and Tier 3 support for infrastructure issues.

Key Responsibilities

### Strategic Oversight

- Execute operational plans and standards set by the Systems Operations Manager.
- Maintain alignment with airport technology roadmap and compliance requirements.

### Operational Excellence

- Administer and maintain Windows servers, endpoints, virtualization platforms, and storage systems.
- Implement automation and scripting for efficiency consistency.
- Ensure lifecycle management for hardware and software.
- Manage and maintain airport-related databases (SQL Server, MySQL) for operational systems such as AODB and reporting tools.
Security Assurance
- Monitor system performance and implement robust security measures.
- Ensure compliance with TSA and Industry security standards.
- Maintain Azure Entra ID security and compliance.
Performance Optimization
- Monitor system performance and implement improvements for scalability and cost efficiency.
- Utilize automation and scripting to streamline processes.
- Optimize database performance and ensure data integrity.

Technical Support & Troubleshooting

- Provide Tier 2 and Tier 3 support for infrastructure issues.
- Troubleshoot database connectivity, query performance, and application integration issues.
- Coordinate with vendors and internal teams for the resolution of complex problems.

Team Leadership

- Mentor junior administrators and service desk staff.
- Foster a collaborative, high-performance team culture.

Additional Responsibilities

- Maintain accurate system documentation and operational procedures.
- Support airport-specific systems (AODB, CUPPS/CUTE, CCTV, Access Control, Baggage/IoT).
- Participate in change management and project implementations.
