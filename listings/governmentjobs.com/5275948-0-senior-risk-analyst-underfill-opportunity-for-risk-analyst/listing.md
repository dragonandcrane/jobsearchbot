### The Opportunity

The Senior Risk Analyst serves as the City’s technical expert and internal consultant on all matters related to risk management. Operating with a high degree of independence, this position plans, organizes, and directs the City’s comprehensive risk management programs, including general liability, workers’ compensation, employee safety, property damage, insurance administration, and loss control. The role is responsible for evaluating risk exposure, developing mitigation strategies, managing insurance portfolios, and ensuring compliance with federal, state, and local regulations.

The Senior Risk Analyst collaborates closely with City departments, external partners, and the City Attorney’s Office to investigate claims, analyze loss trends, and implement programs that reduce financial exposure and enhance organizational safety. This position also oversees risk financing techniques, maintains risk management information systems, prepares annual reports, and provides expert guidance on indemnification, insurance requirements, and contract language. The role requires strong analytical ability, sound judgment, and the capacity to manage complex, high-impact responsibilities in a dynamic public-sector environment.

### Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is an experienced risk management professional with strong analytical abilities, a deep understanding of public-sector liability and insurance programs, and a proven ability to operate independently as the City’s technical expert. They excel at evaluating claims and loss trends, administering complex insurance portfolios, reviewing contracts and indemnification language, and developing practical strategies to reduce exposure and improve organizational safety. They communicate clearly, translate technical concepts for diverse audiences, and build effective working relationships with departments, legal counsel, brokers, and vendors. The ideal candidate is highly organized, skilled in project management, proficient with risk management information systems and data tools, and brings a continuous-improvement mindset to modernizing processes and strengthening the City’s overall risk posture.
