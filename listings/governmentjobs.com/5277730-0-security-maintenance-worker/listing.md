### Definition

To patrol District facilities to assure safety and security; to perform various emergency maintenance and repair duties; and to perform a variety of duties relative to assigned area of responsibility.

## Examples of Duties

- Patrol School District facilities on foot; check security and security alarm systems; report incidents in accordance with established guidelines.

- Report alarm malfunctions; respond to fire and burglar alarms according to established procedures.

- Review incident reports filed by on-call staff for accuracy and submit to supervisor; prepare and maintain various routine logs, records and reports.

- Contact and cooperate with public safety agencies; provide keys, floor plans and other related items to agencies as required.

- Perform emergency maintenance and repair work as needed; reprogram alarm control panels.

- Construct various District signs such as emergency exit signs, name tags and plastic lami¬nated signs.

- Operate and perform operator's maintenance on a district vehicle to patrol district facilities to assure safety and security.

- Operate a variety of machines and equipment such as two-way radio, power saws, grinder, sander, drill press, bench edgers and sheet metal cutter.

- Perform general clean up at job site; observe and implement proper safety practices.

- Provide assistance in various other areas of maintenance as required; perform semi-skilled to skilled maintenance repair and construction work in the basic trades.

- Provide for proper materials as necessary to accomplish work orders; prepare and maintain records including labor, materials and work orders completed; prepare reports as required.

- Perform related duties and responsibilities as assigned.
