General Definition of Work

The primary purpose of this position is to perform skilled technical work to ensure reliable land and mobile radio communications for 911 Dispatch, Law Enforcement, Emergency Medical Services, Fire Protection Services, Emergency Management, and all other first responders in the county. This includes coordination, design, installation, maintenance, repair, modification, and support of the County's wired and wireless telecommunications networks, equipment, and associated circuits for the transmission, reception, and display of voice, data, and other information for the Pender County 911 Center, Pender County Sheriff’s Office, Pender County Emergency Management, and other designated County departments. Ensures the County radio network and its services are available to all County users 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.

To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to complete the essential functions of the position with or without reasonable accommodations.  Reasonable accommodations may be requested by contacting Pender County Human Resources.
This position is open until filled.

Essential Functions

Performs work on land and mobile communication assets such as infrastructure, base stations, and subscriber units, APCO Project 25 (P25) digital trunked systems, digital and analog simulcast radio systems, handheld (portable) and vehicle mounted (mobile) radio programming, and programming (“template”) development. If necessary, arrange for outside vendor assistance.
Provide technical hardware and software support to the radio system’s local area networks (LANs), wide area networks (WANs), servers, and/or workstation computers.
Assists users with the evaluation, selection, acquisition, and installation of hardware, software, and communications packages; advise users in related technical areas leading to improved productivity; and assists in problem analysis and support.
Acts as the county Point of Contact (POC) for the North Carolina State Highway Patrol (NCSHP) Voice Interoperability Plan for Emergency Responders (VIPER) statewide P25 trunked radio system. The POC is responsible for issuing and maintaining all VIPER radio IDs within the county.
Oversees communications security to include maintaining equipment used to protect the radio system from outside penetration and maintaining a security policy to safeguard access to radio network data and resources.
Maintains records of communications equipment, maintenance, and support contracts.
Documents and maintains a current inventory of all Pender County Sheriff’s Office communications equipment, mobile and portable radios. Maintain annual maintenance policies.
Communicates regularly with vendors and suppliers to resolve problems and to recommend services needed.
During emergency/disaster situation, serves under the Logistic Support Branch of the Pender County Emergency Operations Center to coordinate the installation, maintenance, and repair of a variety of communications equipment, including mobile radios, base stations, transmitting towers, HVAC units and generators; assist in the mechanical and operational readiness of the county and to do related work as required.
Performs other duties as assigned.

Knowledge, Skills, Abilities, Education & Experience

Knowledge of computers, peripherals, and software applications; computer equipment utilization principles; computer operating systems and networking principles; multiple system support. Skilled in using standard office equipment; operating computers, peripherals and software applications; troubleshooting hardware and software problems; detecting and reporting communications system failures; installing and maintaining mobile and portable radio peripherals; maintaining reports and files; gathering information; prioritizing work and projects; evaluating new communications equipment; analyzing data and drawing conclusions; translating and converting data; training users; and communication, interpersonal skills as applied to interaction with coworkers, supervisor, the general public, etc. sufficient to exchange or convey information and to receive work direction.

Must obtain and maintain certificates of training for ICS 100, 200, 300, 400, 700, and 800. Within twelve (12) months of appointment (subject to course availability).

Must obtain and maintain completion of FEMA Professional Development Series. Within twelve (12) months of appointment (subject to course availability).

Open an All-Hazard Communication Unit Technician task book under NCAHIMT. Within twelve (12) months of appointment (subject to course availability) and completing within thirty-six (36) months of opening.

Open an All-Hazard Communication Unit Leader task book under NCAHIMT. Within thirty-six (36) months of appointment (subject to course availability) and completing within thirty-six (36) months of opening.

Must obtain and maintain an AVTEC Scout System Administration and Maintenance.
Certification within twelve (12) months of appointment.
Graduation from high school or GED. Graduation from an accredited college or university with an Associate’s Degree in a Technology field such as Electronics, Communications, Information, or related Technology field (or any combination of education, training, and experience equivalent to a two-year course of study in Electronics, Communications, Information, or related Technology field), and Extensive (5+ years) experience in the installation and/or maintenance in at least one of the following: wired or wireless interconnection technologies such as point-to-point or point-to-multipoint microwave networks, Land Mobile Radio networks (APCO Project 25 (P25) preferred), and/or other electronic communications systems.

Valid driver’s license in the State of North Carolina.

This work requires the occasional exertion of up to 50 pounds of force; work regularly requires sitting, speaking or hearing and using hands to handle or feel, frequently requires reaching with hands and arms and occasionally requires standing, walking, climbing or balancing, stooping, kneeling, crouching or crawling, pushing or pulling, lifting and repetitive motions; vocal communication is required for expressing or exchanging ideas by means of the spoken word; hearing is required to perceive information at normal spoken word levels; work requires preparing and analyzing written or computer data, visual inspection involving small defects and/or small parts, using of measuring devices, assembly or fabrication of parts within arm's length, operating machines ,operating motor vehicles or equipment and observing general surroundings and activities; work occasionally requires exposure to the risk of electrical shock; work is generally in a moderately noisy location (e.g. business office, light traffic).
