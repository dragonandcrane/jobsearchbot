### The Position:



### The Opportunity:

Are you a detail-oriented cybersecurity professional who thrives on protecting systems, mitigating risks, and ensuring organizational resilience? The Inland Empire Utilities Agency (IEUA) is seeking a skilled and motivated Information Security Administrator to join our forward-thinking Information Technology team. In this vital role, you’ll play a key part in strengthening the Agency’s Information Security posture by monitoring and responding to cyber threats, administering security tools and processes, supporting governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) functions, and advancing security awareness across the organization. Your expertise will help ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the Agency’s critical systems and data. The ideal candidate is proactive, analytical, and passionate about cybersecurity. Someone who can administer security tools, assess vulnerabilities, respond to incidents, and guide others in maintaining secure technology practices. If you’re ready to take ownership of safeguarding systems, managing risk, and building a strong security culture, this opportunity is for you.

### Education, Training, And Experience:

Graduation from an accredited four-year college or university with major coursework in cybersecurity, information technology, or a closely related field; and at least five years of progressively responsible professional-level experience in information security governance, information security programs, tools and technologies including network monitoring, and vulnerability management; or an equivalent combination of education, training, and experience.
Experience managing enterprise systems and platforms such as Microsoft 365, Azure, Windows/Linux servers, Active Directory, and core security tools including firewalls, SIEM, VPNs, and endpoint protection. Skilled in threat detection, network analysis, and responding to security alerts. Experience supporting security for Operational Technology (OT) or Industrial Control System (ICS) environments is desirable including participating in simulated cyber-attacks (red team) and defense (blue team) scenarios.

### Skills and Abilities

Ability to identify, assess, and mitigate information security risks; analyze and respond effectively to cybersecurity incidents; and apply sound judgment in prioritizing and implementing remediation strategies.
Skill in developing, implementing, and maintaining security policies, standards, and procedures in alignment with organizational goals, regulatory requirements, and industry best practices.
Strong ability to use cybersecurity tools and technologies for monitoring, threat detection, vulnerability management, and forensic analysis; skilled in evaluating complex technical issues and developing practical, secure solutions.
Excellent ability to convey technical security concepts to non-technical audiences, collaborate with cross-functional teams, and lead engaging security awareness and training initiatives across the organization.

### About Us

The Inland Empire Utilities Agency is a regional wastewater treatment agency and wholesale distributor of imported water to approximately 950,000 people across 242-square miles in western San Bernardino County. Under the leadership of a directly elected five-member Board of Directors, the Agency is committed to supporting the needs of its service area and safeguarding public health through significant investments in a diverse water supply portfolio, reliable municipal/industrial wastewater collection and treatment services, and other related utility services in a regionally planned and cost-effective manner.
As a member agency of the Metropolitan Water District of Southern California (Metropolitan), IEUA provides supplemental water supplies, primarily via the State Water Project (SWP) for distribution among nine retail agencies. IEUA also replenishes local groundwater supplies with captured stormwater and recycled water produced by IEUA that is later extracted by local water agencies for use as a drinking water supply.
