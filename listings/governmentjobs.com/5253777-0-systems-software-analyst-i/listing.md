## The Position

Bargaining Unit: Local 21 - Non-Supervisory Management

Why join Contra Costa Health?

Contra Costa is one of the few counties in the nation to offer a full spectrum of health-related services that cover health at every level: the individual, the family, and the community. In addition to the Contra Costa Regional Medical Center and various Health Clinics, the Health Services department is composed of several other divisions, including Behavioral Health, Contra Costa Health Plan, Emergency Medical Services, Environmental Health, Hazardous Materials, Homeless Housing and Health, and Public Health. As a public health system, we are dedicated to improving the health and wellness of all residents in our communities.

Contra Costa Health has an excellent employment opportunity for one (1) full-time Systems Software Analyst I position assigned primarily to the Health Services Information Technology Unit located in Concord, CA.

### We are looking for someone who

- Is articulate, both verbally and in writing, communicates clearly and professionally with staff, providers, and the vendor community and works well in a team.
- Excels at troubleshooting and shows initiative and follow-through.
- Is a responsible innovator who effectively analyzes and prioritizes situations as they arise during the day, responding to urgent requests in a timely manner in an atmosphere with competing demands.
- Acknowledges and respects cultural and linguistic differences of diverse populations.
- Demonstrates empathy in handling customer needs and builds a trusting rapport with customers.
- Brings an analytical mindset and curiosity to deliver well-defined outcomes.

### What you will typically be responsible for

- Developing software development practices with our cloud service deployments.
- Producing and managing documentation for our processes.
- Administration of cloud-hosted software solutions and integrations.
- Administration of Identity management solutions and integrations.
- Supporting end users with SaaS application issues.
- Project tracking using Agile/Scrum methodologies.

- The team you would work with is full of people who are passionate about their jobs and constantly innovating.

## Minimum Qualifications

### Education

Possession of a bachelor's degree from an accredited college or university which included completion of 30 semester hours in computer science, information processing, or a closely related field.

### Experience

One (1) year full-time, or its equivalent, experience in the design, implementation, and maintenance of operating systems software on complex multi-user systems

### And

two (2) years full-time or its equivalent programming experience on multi-user systems. At least one (1) year of the applications programming must have been in a higher level applications language (e.g., COBOL, C, or C++);

### Or

three (3) years full-time or its equivalent experience performing applications programming on multiple user systems which included two (2) years programming in a higher level applications language.

### Substitution for Education

Additional applications programming experience in a higher level applications language or systems software experience of the type noted above may be substituted for the required education on a year-for-year basis up to a maximum of two (2) years. No substitution is allowed for the required 30 semester hours in computer science, information processing, or a closely related field.

### Desirable Qualifications

- Experience supporting cloud-hosted environments
- Vendor certifications for cloud related technologies
- Experience deploying and supporting XaaS technologies like Microsoft 365, Jira, Pagerduty, etc.
- Experience with Identity management solutions and product integrations
- Software development pipelines and associated tooling like Git, Ansible, Postman, Terraform, etc.
- Experience leveraging API integrations between platforms

### Conviction History

After you receive a conditional job offer, you will be fingerprinted, and your fingerprints will be sent to the California Department of Justice (DOJ) and the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI). The resulting report of your conviction history (if any) will be used to determine whether the nature of your conviction conflicts with the specific duties and responsibilities of the job for which you have received a conditional job offer. If a conflict exists, you will be asked to present any evidence of rehabilitation that may mitigate the conflict, except when federal or state regulations bar employment in specific circumstances. Having a conviction history does not automatically preclude you from a job with Contra Costa County. If you accept a conditional job offer, the Human Resources Department will contact you to schedule a fingerprinting appointment.
