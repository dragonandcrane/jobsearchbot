Job Description
Senior Marketing Specialist – Promotions

### Summary

Under general direction, leads, coordinates, and manages innovative marketing programs. Develops and implements innovative marketing programs with emphasis on research, strategic planning, execution, problem solving and evaluation.
This is an exempt position in salary grade 190: Min – $84,531.20 | Mid – $100,984.00 | Max – $117,416.00/year. The starting salary will be within this range based on qualifications.
This posting will remain open filled.

### What You’ll Do

Lead and manage innovative marketing programs from concept to evaluation
Conduct market research and analyze data to inform strategy and decision-making
Develop and execute strategic marketing plans aligned with organizational goals
Coordinate cross-functional projects, ensuring timelines and deliverables are met
Create compelling messaging and campaigns across multiple channels
Monitor performance metrics and optimize campaigns for maximum impact
Identify opportunities, solve problems creatively, and continuously improve results

### What We're Looking For

Bachelor’s degree in public transportation, marketing, communications, public relations, business administration, or a related field
Minimum of five (5) years of professional experience demonstrating strong marketing and project management capabilities
Proven ability to develop and implement successful marketing strategies
Strong research, analytical, and problem-solving skills
Excellent communication and collaboration abilities
Highly organized with the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously
Creative mindset with a results-driven approach
Special Requirements
Valid California driver license required.
