Job Description

GIS Analyst / Associate – Geographic Information Systems

Under general direction, the GIS Analyst/Associate coordinates, designs, develops, maintains, and documents Geographic Information System (GIS) databases, applications, and products that support OCTA’s transportation programs and projects. This role plays a key part in delivering spatial analysis, data visualization, and innovative geospatial solutions that inform planning, programming, and operational decision-making.

The GIS Analyst is an exempt position in salary grade 195: Min $88,753.60 – Mid $106,038.40 – Max $123,302.40/annually; the GIS Analyst Associate is a non-exempt position in salary grade 185:  Min $38.71 – Mid $46.24 – Max $53.76/hour. The starting salary will be within the established range based on qualifications.

This posting will remain open until filled.

What You’ll Do

- Design, develop, integrate, and maintain complex geographic databases and related reporting tools
- Perform spatial analyses and develop GIS models to support transportation planning and operations
- Prepare high-quality maps, charts, dashboards, and other geospatial products
- Edit, clean, and prepare GIS data for analysis and reporting using industry-standard tools
- Assist in developing and hosting web-based GIS services and geoprocessing applications
- Support and promote an integrated transportation GIS database across departments
- Provide technical support and guidance on GIS software and applications
- Coordinate with consultants and ensure adherence to scope, schedule, and budget requirements
- Represent OCTA in meetings with public agencies, consultants, and professional organizations
- Prepare written reports and presentations, including materials for executive leadership or Board meetings

What We’re Looking For

- Bachelor’s degree in Geography, GIS, Planning, Engineering, Statistics, Information Technology, or related field
- Minimum of three years of GIS-related professional experience (Associate level: experience preferred)
- Strong understanding of spatial analysis and geographic data processing
- Experience developing GIS databases, applications, and analytical tools
- Proficiency in GIS software and related technologies
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, and data visualization skills
- Excellent written and verbal communication abilities
- Master’s degree and advanced GIS application experience (preferred)

Why You’ll Love It Here

- Apply cutting-edge GIS tools to real-world transportation challenges
- Support major transportation planning, programming, and operational initiatives
- Collaborate across departments and partner agencies
- Contribute to data-driven decision-making that shapes regional mobility
- Competitive compensation, comprehensive benefits, and professional growth opportunities
