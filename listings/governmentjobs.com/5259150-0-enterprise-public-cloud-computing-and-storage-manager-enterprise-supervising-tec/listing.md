## About the Department

The City of San José innovates to provide exceptional civic services using advanced technologies to help our community thrive.

As one of the largest cities in the nation, the City manages a large set of services and assets and operates on a budget of $6.2 billion, with approximately 7,000 employees, serving about 1 million residents and 80,000 businesses in the heart of Silicon Valley.

The Information Technology Department’s (ITD) mission is to enrich the quality of life in San José through innovation, collaboration, and engagement. ITD enables that mission through business and infrastructure systems, cybersecurity, data management and analysis, responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI), productivity and collaboration tools, the San José 311 resident experience platform, data equity and privacy programs, and strategic planning. San José is powered by truly great people, a robust technology environment, and a strong sense of purpose.

The IT department is a leader in innovation, embracing cutting-edge technologies and pioneering solutions to enhance efficiency and quality of life in San José. As part of this effort, the City leads a national initiative for AI through the GovAI Coalition, which was established to give local governments a voice in shaping the future of AI, ensuring it is developed responsibly for the public good.

Promoting the City’s commitment to equity and inclusion, we believe that all members of the community, regardless of background, have access to the tools and resources needed to thrive in the digital age. San José is located in the heart of Silicon Valley, which boasts a rich history in technology, education, and agriculture. Over half of San José residents speak a language other than English at home, highlighting the importance of language accessibility in all City services. By fostering inclusivity, promoting digital literacy, and building accessible platforms, we are advancing technology while creating a more equitable future for everyone.

## Position Duties

The first round of application review was on Tuesday, March 24, 2026.
This position will remain open until filled and applications are reviewed continuously. We encourage applicants to apply as soon as possible as this recruitment may close at any time.

The City of San José is recruiting for an Enterprise Public Cloud Computing and Storage Manager (Enterprise Supervising Technology Analyst) in the Information Technology Department. As a technical manager, this position will be responsible for overseeing infrastructure services and day-to-day operations.

The City of San José believes in the model of utilizing technology as a means for increased optimization as well as cost savings. With a significant technology refresh and upgrade in the works, there is a greater need for highly skilled, hands-on technical leadership, infrastructure service oversight, strategic thinking, and effective technical project management.

This position requires strong technical hands-on experience and leadership skills, as well as the ability to take initiative and direct others. We are seeking an individual with a willingness to adapt to an ever-changing business as well as an ever-changing technical environment.

The ideal candidate must be able to take full ownership of design, implement, and manage cloud infrastructure operations, and lead projects to completion. Infrastructure operations include cloud infrastructure (Azure / AWS / GCP), server virtualization, cloud storage architectures, FileShare service, backup service, system management, administration, automation, and monitoring, etc. This position demands a proven ability to resolve complex technical issues with limited resources. A team leader and player with a positive attitude are essential to this position.

### Duties may include but are not limited to

- Provide technical leadership for the City’s enterprise infrastructure, which includes Public Cloud (Azure) governance and operations, distributed file systems (and/or FileShare), enterprise cloud storage, system management and monitoring, cloud administration, enterprise system backups and restore, automation, and Disaster Recovery of infrastructure services.
- Design and implement Azure cloud backup and disaster recovery.
- Monitor and optimize cloud infrastructure for cost, performance, and reliability.
- Perform hands-on system configuration and advanced troubleshooting of infrastructure issues to ensure service availability and meet service level expectations.
- Improve service levels, availability, and cost efficiency through automation and proactive service management.
- Develop technical guidelines and governance frameworks to ensure best practices and security.
- Lead project implementation to completion.
- Enforce data integrity, availability across cloud platforms.
- Coordinate communication and IT service needs with departments.
- Maintain a 5-year portfolio strategy for the service area.
- Manage enterprise asset life cycle, including tracking, replacement, and surplus of equipment.
- Manage enterprise infrastructure maintenance and support contracts.
- Provide technical leadership and mentoring to subordinates. Ensure the team meets and exceeds performance expectations defined through metrics and benchmarks.
- Manage team resources, employee work plans, and performance appraisals.

Please note that the Enterprise Public Cloud Computing and Storage Manager position is currently eligible for a hybrid telework schedule. The schedule for working remotely and onsite is subject to change. The City is currently on a 32-hour onsite workweek.

## Minimum Qualifications

### Education and Experience

A Bachelor’s Degree from an accredited college or university in a relevant field, AND four (4) years of progressively responsible professional/journey level experience, of which at least two (2) years of experience include lead technical work in development, implementation and maintenance of electronic business systems/solutions, or application development and/or support.

### Acceptable Substitution

- Additional years of increasingly responsible directly related work experience may be substituted for education on a year-for-year basis up to two (2) years.
- Completion of a Master's Degree in a relevant field from an accredited college or university may be substituted for one (1) year of the required two (2) years of experience which include lead technical work in development, implementation and maintenance of electronic business systems/solutions or application development and/or support.

Required Licensing (such as driver’s license, certifications, etc.):

- Possession of a valid State of California driver’s license may be required.
- Certification as a Microsoft Certified Systems Engineer (MCSE) or equivalent certification from a professional organization acceptable to the City of San Jose may be required.

Passing a San Jose Police Department background check is a condition of employment.
