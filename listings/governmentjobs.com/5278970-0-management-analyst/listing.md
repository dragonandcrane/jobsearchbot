### The Position

The Management Analyst plays a key role in driving City initiatives forward, tackling complex challenges, and helping shape how local government operates day to day. This position offers the opportunity to work on high-impact projects across departments—conducting research, developing recommendations, supporting policy and budget decisions, and collaborating with leadership, elected officials, and community stakeholders. It’s a fast-paced, high-visibility role for someone who enjoys variety, problem-solving, and making a meaningful impact in public service.

This is the journey-level classification in the Management Analyst series, where individuals operate with a high degree of independence and ownership. Analysts at this level manage a full range of assignments—from analytical projects to program coordination—while leading initiatives and contributing directly to organizational priorities.

### The Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is a highly organized, analytically driven professional who thrives in a fast-paced environment and is motivated by improving how local government operates. This individual brings experience in grant management, research, policy and legislative analysis, and program coordination, and is comfortable working independently with minimal supervision. They communicate clearly and effectively with a wide range of stakeholders—including department heads, consultants, elected officials, and grantor agencies—and can translate complex information into concise staff reports, agenda items, and presentations for City Council and committees.

The successful candidate is proficient in data management, performance tracking, and grant compliance, with a working knowledge of municipal budgeting and applicable federal and state regulations. This role also supports day-to-day operations and provides administrative coordination for projects, including support for public Council committee meetings. Experience in contract administration, grant research, and legislative analysis is highly desirable. A bachelor’s degree in public administration, business administration, or a related field, along with three years of progressively responsible analytical or program support experience in a public agency or similar organization, is required. Above all, the candidate is a proactive self-starter who exercises sound judgment, collaborates effectively, and brings practical, solutions-oriented thinking to support the City’s strategic goals.

## EXPERIENCE AND TRAINING GUIDELINES

### Education and Experience

Equivalent to a bachelor’s degree in public administration, business administration, or a related field, and three years of increasingly responsible administrative, analytical, or program support experience in a public agency or similar organization.

### Knowledge, Skills, and Abilities

### Knowledge of

- Principles and practices of public administration and municipal operations.

- Administrative, budgetary, and contract management procedures.

- Methods of research, analysis, and report preparation.

- Office management principles, including workflow coordination and recordkeeping.

- Agenda preparation, legislative tracking, and public meeting procedures.

- Principles of customer service and public communication.

- English usage, spelling, grammar, and punctuation.

- Modern office practices, procedures, and software applications.

- Pertinent federal, state, and local laws, regulations, and ordinances.

- City operations, programs, and functions.

- Applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations.

- Effective communication and presentation techniques.

- Grant research, writing, and post-award reporting

### Ability to

- Perform responsible administrative and analytical duties with minimal supervision.

- Perform professional analytical work with initiative and minimal supervision.

- Evaluate issues, identify solutions, and present clear recommendations.

- Prepare and present comprehensive reports, policies, and procedures.

- Analyze, interpret, and apply laws and regulations.

- Coordinate and manage projects and meet deadlines.

- Communicate effectively with staff, management, officials, and the public.

- Establish and maintain cooperative working relationships.

- Exercise sound judgment and maintain confidentiality.
