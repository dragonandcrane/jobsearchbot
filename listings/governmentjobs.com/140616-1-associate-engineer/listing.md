The City of Santa Paula is hiring an Associate Engineer to join its Public Works team. This role involves supporting the City’s Capital Improvement Program (CIP), reviewing engineering plans and permits, performing field inspections and assisting with streets, water, wastewater and recycled water projects.

The position offers hands-on experience in municipal engineering and the chance to contribute to meaningful infrastructure improvements in a Ventura County community. The ideal candidate is eager to learn, collaborative and committed to delivering high-quality public works projects.
