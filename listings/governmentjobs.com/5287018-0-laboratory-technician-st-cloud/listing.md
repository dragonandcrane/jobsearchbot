Job Posting

The Laboratory Technician serves as a member of the Public Utilities Water Quality Team and completes a variety of analyses to verify that water quality meets and exceeds state and federal requirements. This position requires application of skilled technical analytical techniques, effective communication, high levels of data integrity and efficient time management. The Laboratory Technician may be assigned to either of the Department’s certified laboratories.
