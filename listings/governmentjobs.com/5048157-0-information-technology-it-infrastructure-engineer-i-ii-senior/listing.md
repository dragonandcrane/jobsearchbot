The Position

You will also serve as an escalation point for the IT Helpdesk, assisting with complex technical issues that go beyond first-level support and ensuring timely resolution of infrastructure-related incidents and requests.

### Job Summary

Under general direction from the Information Technology (IT) Services Operations Manager, develop, enhance and support infrastructure and systems for IT Services customers. Guide the implementation and use of new and appropriate information technology in the most efficient and cost-effective manner.

Provide information systems technical support and training to IT staff and customers. Provide leadership and inter departmental communication during analysis and design, implementation, and maintenance phases. Review, analyze and provide recommendations for system integrations.

Troubleshoot and maintain IT infrastructure, including network hardware and software. Provide, system configuration and solution integration services, assisting in ensuring functional and/or technical integration with various District systems and business processes.

### Distinguishing Characteristics

IT Infrastructure Engineer I
is the entry-level class in the IT Infrastructure Engineer series. The IT Infrastructure Engineer I is assigned to work on projects under guidance of experienced IT Infrastructure Engineers. Positions assigned to this class will usually perform daily administration of systems, network, and infrastructure in addition to responsibilities related to system and network security. This position is not expected to function at the same skill level as senior positions and will usually exercise less independent direction and decision making.

IT Infrastructure Engineer II
is the professional level class in the IT Infrastructure Engineer series. The IT Infrastructure Engineer II is expected to perform duties associated with all prior position levels as necessary. Positions assigned to this classification are distinguished from the IT Infrastructure Engineer I by a proven track record of successfully completed projects that they have led or provided a leadership role within their functional area. Positions assigned to this level

are expected to mentor and train staff on fundamental concepts related to servers, networking, infrastructure, and security systems. In addition, positions assigned to this class are responsible for hardware and software procurements, as well as IT planning, development and system and network design.

Senior IT Infrastructure Engineer
is the advanced professional class in the IT Infrastructure Engineer series. The Senior IT Infrastructure Engineer is expected to perform duties associated with all prior position levels as necessary. Positions assigned to this classification are distinguished from the IT Infrastructure Engineer II classification by their demonstrated performance of complex database administration, along with the analysis and design of network and infrastructure systems. In addition, positions assigned to this class will have a proven track record of successfully managing and completing projects in which they have demonstrated a significant leadership role. The individual will have a proven track record of mentoring and training staff in advanced systems, network and infrastructure concepts, in addition to other relevant technical areas.  In some cases, these individuals may assume a leadership role, overseeing and directing the work of other IT staff.

## Examples of Duties

### Duties And Responsibilities

Provide strategic planning, implementation, and ongoing support for IT systems. networks and infrastructure. Responsibilities include server and network administration, infrastructure management, cloud services (PaaS & SaaS), datacenter operations, virtualization platforms, database administration and phone system support.

Analyze existing operations and evaluate both new and existing technologies to recommend and implement process improvements that enhance efficiency, strengthen security and reduce or mitigate risks.

Provide technical support by utilizing knowledge of existing technologies to troubleshoot issues, resolve problems and support the implementation of new projects.

Keep abreast of emerging trends and best practices in server, database, network, datacenter and security concepts and technologies.

Monitor and optimize server, network and system performance. Collaborate with IT Security Division team to remediate and mitigate security vulnerabilities across all infrastructure and network environments as needed or upon request.

Demonstrate the ability to deploy, manage and maintain systems and their corresponding or associated software, including firewalls, intrusion prevention systems, and anti-virus and endpoint protection software, patch management, system monitoring solutions, and backup and recovery systems, as necessary to ensure security, performance and reliability.

Develop, implement, maintain, and oversee enforcement of policies, procedures and associated plans for network and system administration, as well as user access, ensuring compliance with industry best practices and security standards.

Participate in the planning, design, and implementation of disaster recovery and/or emergency response plans. Provide assistance to the Security and Emergency Preparedness Department as necessary.

Comply with all District rules, regulations, policies and procedures.

Must satisfy any existing and future District security clearance or background check requirements.

Perform other related duties as required or assigned by supervisor.

All levels of the IT Infrastructure Engineer series are required to provide 24/7 support as assigned.

## Typical Qualifications

### Qualifications

Any combination of experience and education that would likely provide the required skills and abilities is qualifying. A typical way to obtain the skills and abilities would be:

Education

Usually exhibited by a person with a Bachelor’s Degree in computer information systems, computer science or related field is preferred for the IT Infrastructure Engineer I. A Bachelor’s degree in computer information systems, computer science or related field is required for the IT Infrastructure Engineer II and Senior IT Infrastructure Engineer levels.

Experience

IT Infrastructure Engineer I

None required. Relevant experience in system and infrastructure administration including exposure to servers, networks, virtualization is highly desirable.

IT Infrastructure Engineer II

Four (4) or more years of experience at the IT Infrastructure Engineer I level in technology areas related to systems, servers, networking, datacenter, user and security administration, in addition to project management. Individuals in the IT Infrastructure Engineer II level will have experience with systems analysis, systems integration, system performance and system monitoring, in addition to staff mentoring and training.

Senior IT Infrastructure Engineer

Six (6) or more years of experience at the IT Infrastructure Engineer level in areas related to systems, networking, security administration and/or database management, in addition to project management. Individuals in the Senior IT Infrastructure Engineer level will have experience with operations and security administration, policy development, strategic planning, systems analysis and integration, performance monitoring, security log analysis, computer forensics, and database management and demonstrated success in mentoring and developing junior staff.

Skills and Abilities

Interpersonal and Communication.
Ability to communicate clearly and concisely, both orally and in writing with both internal and external contacts; and work cooperatively with executive and management staff, employees and others to provide effective information technology services support at agreed-upon support levels. Maintain a positive work atmosphere by acting and communicating in a manner so that you get along with customers, clients, co-workers and management.

Technical and Analytical.
Knowledge of systems analysis, programming and testing procedures utilizing District standard computing platforms; methods practices and principles involved in maintaining an effective hardware and software platform; work on multiple tasks concurrently; analyze and resolve IT Services system problems; and properly interpret various situations in accordance with existing laws, rules, regulations and policies.

Administration and Operations.
Knowledge of operation and care of general-purpose computer equipment; and principles of project management. Ability to work in a rapidly changing work environment and to work well under pressure; maintain work quality while meeting reasonable deadlines; refine organizational skills and work independently; and direct the work of internal and external project team members as part of project management responsibility.
