The Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate for the Systems Administrator position is a highly experienced professional with expert-level skills managing both on-premises and virtualized environments. They bring extensive hands-on experience with VMware virtualization platforms, Veeam backup and recovery solutions, Microsoft technologies such as Windows Server and Active Directory, and core networking infrastructure. This individual demonstrates exceptional attention to detail and a strong ability to troubleshoot complex technical issues, identify root causes, and implement reliable, long-term solutions that maintain system stability, security, and performance.

In addition to strong technical expertise, the candidate shows initiative and works effectively with limited supervision while confidently prioritizing workload across operational needs, projects, and incident response. They possess excellent soft skills and serve as a team lead who collaborates well with colleagues, mentors junior staff, and communicates clearly with both technical and non-technical stakeholders. The ideal professional is trustworthy and discreet, capable of handling sensitive systems, credentials, and organizational information with the highest level of professionalism.

The Region

Mono County is a rural region with a dynamic community of 13,000 residents located in the eastern side of California’s Sierra Nevada range. Development is increasing in the area due to the consistent growth of the town of Mammoth Lakes. Known as “California’s Great Beyond” this natural playground offers a complete range of amenities and activities making it an ideal place to live and work. Enjoy fine shops, restaurants, and cafes. Snowboard, ski, or bike down mountain trails, fish for trout, explore the backcountry, play a round of golf, relax in soothing hot springs, or paddle a kayak along the shores of Mono Lake.

The town of Mammoth Lakes is the most populated region of Mono County, hosting Mammoth Mountain Ski area and various resort-community amenities. On the quieter side, June Lake, Lee Vining, Bridgeport, Walker, Coleville, Chalfant, and Benton offer a more rural atmosphere, unlimited outdoor adventures, and small-town charm.

## Additional Information

Minimum Qualifications

Any combination of training and experience which would likely provide the required knowledge and abilities is qualifying. A typical way to obtain the required knowledge and abilities would be:

- A Bachelor’s degree from an accredited college or university in a computer related field OR four (4) years of experience working with and administering systems.

### Or

- Current MCSA with four (4) years of experience, an MCSE with five (5) years of experience, or an MCITP with six (6) years of experience working in some level of system administration.
- Completed coursework in systems design is highly desirable.
